Who We Are

Stillwater Spray Foam and Construction LLC is dedicated to delivering high-quality insulation solutions for residential and commercial properties. We work with homeowners, builders, and contractors who want reliable, long-lasting performance from their insulation systems.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple:

To provide efficient, environmentally friendly insulation solutions that protect structures from heat, cold, moisture, and pests while maximizing energy performance.

What We Do

We specialize in spray foam insulation using the latest technology to create airtight, energy-efficient spaces. Our work helps regulate temperature, improve indoor comfort, and increase the overall durability of your structure.

Who We Serve

We work with:

  • Homeowners building New Homes
  • Barndominium Projects
  • Shop and Barn Owners
  • Contractors and Builders
  • Commercial Property Owners

Why It Matters

  • Proper insulation isn’t just about comfort — it’s about protecting your investment. Our systems help reduce energy costs, prevent moisture damage, and create healthier indoor environments.
