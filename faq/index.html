Everything You Need to Know About Spray Foam & Our Services

At Stillwater Spray Foam and Construction LLC, we believe informed customers make confident decisions. Here are answers to some of the most common questions we receive.

Spray foam insulation is a high-performance material that expands when applied, filling gaps, cracks, and hard-to-reach areas. It creates an airtight seal that improves energy efficiency, indoor comfort, and overall building performance.

Open Cell Foam:

  • Softer and more flexible
  • Ideal for interior walls and ceilings
  • Great for sound reduction
  • More budget-friendly

Closed Cell Foam:

  • Denser and more rigid
  • Higher insulation value (R-value)
  • Acts as a moisture barrier
  • Adds structural strength

It depends on your building type, budget, and goals. Open cell is great for interior applications and sound control, while closed cell is best for maximum insulation, moisture resistance, and durability. We can help you choose the right option for your project.

Spray foam insulation is extremely durable and can last for decades when properly installed. It does not sag or settle like traditional insulation materials.
