Spray Foam Insulation

Spray foam insulation is one of the most effective ways to seal and protect your building. It expands to fill gaps, cracks, and hard-to-reach areas, creating an airtight barrier that improves energy efficiency and indoor comfort.

Benefits:

  • Reduces energy costs
  • Improves indoor temperature control
  • Seals air leaks
  • Helps prevent moisture buildup
  • Adds long-term durability

Open Cell Spray Foam

Open cell foam is a lightweight, flexible insulation option ideal for interior applications.

Best For:

  • Walls and ceilings
  • Soundproofing
  • Budget-conscious projects

Advantages:

  • Expands to fill tight spaces
  • Excellent noise reduction
  • Cost-effective solution

Closed Cell Spray Foam

Closed cell foam is a high-density insulation that provides superior performance and protection.

Best For:

  • Exterior walls
  • Metal buildings
  • High-moisture areas

Advantages:

  • Higher R-value per inch
  • Acts as a moisture barrier
  • Adds structural strength
  • Long-lasting and durable
